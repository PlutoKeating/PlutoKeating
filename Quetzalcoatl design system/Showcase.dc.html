01 — Art direction · 艺术指导

Ten o’clock sun

Every surface in the system is lit by one sun sitting at ten o’clock. Highlights sit on top edges, shadows fall to the lower right, floating things are brighter than what lies beneath them. Five signatures follow from that one idea.

S—1

The nose corner

Plates are square at 2px with exactly one large rounded corner, top right. Never four equal radii on a container.

S—2

Keys and keylines

Anything you press is a capsule or a circle with a 1.5px ink keyline and 2px of real travel. Press one.

S—3

Two grounds

Stone Charcoal is the structure. Warm Ivory paper and full colour fields are laid over it at large scale, so charcoal never fills more than about half of a page.

S—4

Floating layers are sunlit

Menus, popovers, toasts and tooltips are ivory resin sheets. Dialogs and drawers are paper. The higher a layer, the more light it catches.

Eject tray
S—5

Clear type, one italic voice

A plain, open grotesque for statements, a mono for facts, and a serif italic reserved for one phrase per view. Legibility comes before character.

02 — Design philosophy · 设计哲学

Seven rules for building tomorrow

1

Light comes from somewhere

One light source. If a shadow, highlight or gradient has no light logic, remove it.

2

Colour at full strength

Accents are used at token value in large flat fields. Never grey them down to look retro.

3

Controls are objects

Keys travel, switches slide, trays eject. Feedback is physical and arrives in under 100ms.

4

One loud thing per view

A headline, a numeral or a colour field may be oversized. Everything else stays quiet and ruled.

5

Rules before boxes

Separate with hairlines, 2px rules and space first. Use a card only when content must move as a unit.

6

Every mark has a job

No decorative labels, ticks, grids or icons. If removing it loses no information, remove it.

7

Readable by anyone

4.5:1 text contrast, a visible focus ring on every control, and colour is never the only signal.

我们怀念的不是昨天，而是昨天曾经相信的明天。

05 — Graphic language · 图形语言

Sun, horizon, speed

Fig. 05 — Composite 01 nostalgia for tomorrow
Sun disc
A flat circle, always cropped by an edge or a horizon. One per composition. Amber or orange only.
Horizon
A full-width 2px rule that things rise from or rest on. It opens sections and anchors empty states.
Speed bars
Three bars stepping down in length from the left margin. They imply direction; never use more than three.
Nose plate
The container shape. The rounded corner always points up and to the right, toward where things are going.
Halftone & pinstripe
Print textures for one large field per view. They sit on colour, never behind body text.
07
Oversized numerals
Indices and real quantities set very large in the display weight. Only numbers that mean something.
Composition A — 7 / 5 split

Columns are unequal. The heavy side holds one statement; the light side holds the detail.

Composition B — hanging headline

Display type hangs from a rule on the left margin; body starts from the centre line.

Photography

Daylight, real objects, warm colour. Duotone is greyscale multiplied over amber. Always framed with a keyline and nose corner.

06 — Material & surface · 材质与表面

Four materials, each with a job

M—1 Paint
Paint

Flat, opaque accent colour. For actions, selection and colour fields. No gradient, no gloss.

M—2 Paper
Paper

Warm Ivory with an ink keyline. For reading surfaces, dialogs and drawers.

M—3 Anodized
Anodized

A machined silver band. Only on parts a finger grabs: switch and slider thumbs, chrome icon keys.

M—4 Resin
Resin

Translucent ivory with a blur and a top-edge highlight. Only for layers that float: menus, popovers, toasts, tooltips. Shown here at 72% so the effect is visible; the token is 92%.

Surface rules

  1. Wells go down, keys come upInputs and tracks carry an inset top shadow. Pressables carry a 2px ink underside.
  2. Plates rest flatCards have a hairline and no shadow until they are hovered or dragged.
  3. Edge light, one pixelSolid surfaces catch a 1px sharp highlight on the top edge and a 1px sharp shade below. Never blurred, never coloured.
  4. One soft shadow per objectNeutral, offset to the lower right, with a 1px hard contact line. No glow, no coloured shadow.
  5. Blur is earnedOnly resin sheets and the scrim are translucent. Cards are never glass.

08 — Interaction & motion · 交互与动效

Click, slide, eject

Principles

  1. Borrowed from hardwareKeys travel, thumbs slide, trays eject, LEDs step. If a motion has no mechanical counterpart, it is probably not ours.
  2. Fast in, faster outFeedback under 100ms. Nothing in a task flow takes longer than 360ms. Exits use two thirds of the entry time.
  3. A little overshootArriving objects pass their mark by a hair and settle. That is the only playfulness allowed.
  4. Loading is steppedProgress advances in segments; indicators chase in discrete steps, not smooth spins.
  5. Motion is optionalWith reduced-motion set, every duration collapses and state changes are instant.

States

HoverKeys lighten. Wells gain a strong border. Tabs and cards lift 2px. Nav draws its bar from the left.
Active2px downward travel in 80ms; the underside shadow collapses and an inner shadow appears.
Focus2px ring, 3px offset, keyboard only. Horizon Sky on charcoal, Technical Blue on paper. Never removed.
Loading
Under 1s: nothing. 1–10s: LED chase or stepped bar. Layout known: skeleton with sunbeam.
Feedback
Results in place first (the control changes), then a toast if the result is off-screen or delayed.
